# How claims are curated

A claim is a sentence a reader could act on, attached to one or more regions,
with an honest `evidenceStatus` and at least one `evidence-record` pointing at
a `source` with a resolvable identifier (DOI preferred).

## Rules

1. **One idea per claim.** If a sentence needs "and", split it.
2. **Species is explicit.** A rat finding is a rat claim. If the claim spans
   species, set `species: "multiple"` and let each evidence record carry its own.
3. **Preparation is explicit.** `context.preparation` on the claim and on every
   evidence record: in_vivo, post_mortem, clinical, in_vitro, in_silico, mixed.
4. **No `established` on one source.** Two independent lines of evidence minimum,
   ideally different methods.
5. **Contradicting evidence is recorded, not discarded.** Use `polarity:
   contradicts`. A claim with credible evidence on both sides is `contested`,
   not deleted.
6. **Write the folk version when it is widely believed and wrong.** "The amygdala
   is the fear centre" is in the atlas as a `contested` claim with the evidence
   against it, because readers arrive believing it.
7. **Locators.** Patient initials, figure numbers, or table references go in
   `evidence-record.locator` so a reader can find the finding, not just the paper.
8. **Notes are for caveats**, not summaries: sample size, whether a lesion was
   confined to the region, whether a review is doing the supporting work.

## Lifecycle

- Claims are authored with `status: proposed`.
- A human curator reads every linked source (at least the abstract and the
  relevant figure or table), confirms the statement is what the source says,
  and sets `status: active`.
- If a later source overturns a claim, add the contradicting evidence record,
  lower `evidenceStatus`, and if warranted set the old claim `superseded` with
  `supersededByClaimId` pointing at the replacement. Never edit a claim's
  statement in place once active; supersede it.

## Where claims live

`data/function/curated-claims-v1.json` is generated by
`scripts/curation/gen_claims_v1.py`. Edit the script, regenerate, validate.
The viewer shows claims on the region they are attached to and, in a
separate section, claims attached to containing regions.

## What v1 covers

Twelve claims across hippocampal formation / CA1, area hOc1 (V1), areas 4a/4p
(M1), and the amygdala; 23 sources with verified DOIs; 28 evidence records of
which 5 contradict. Two claims are deliberately `contested` (the continuous
motor homunculus; the amygdala as fear centre).
