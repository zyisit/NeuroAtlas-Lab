# Contributing to NeuroAtlas Lab

NeuroAtlas Lab is under early architecture development.

Before contributing scientific data or claims:

1. Identify the source and version.
2. Record the applicable license.
3. Preserve external identifiers.
4. State species and experimental context when relevant.
5. Distinguish observation, curated information, inference, and model-derived output.
6. Do not introduce clinical or causal claims without appropriate evidence.
7. Prefer machine-readable additions that conform to the schemas in `schemas/`.

Changes to ontology predicates, evidence semantics, or model interpretation should be reviewed before implementation.
