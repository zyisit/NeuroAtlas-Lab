# NeuroAtlas Predicate Vocabulary v0.3

## Anatomy
part_of, contains, adjacent_to, overlaps, located_in

## Connectivity
projects_to, receives_from, connected_to, modulates

## Cellular
contains_cell_type, expresses, derived_from

## Chemistry
releases, binds, activates, inhibits, transports, synthesizes, degrades

## Function
associated_with, contributes_to, required_for, modulates, participates_in, activated_during

## Modeling
represented_by, modeled_by, parameterizes, simulates, produces

## Evidence
supported_by, contradicted_by, derived_from

`causes` is intentionally excluded from the initial vocabulary. Causal claims require explicit scientific-governance review.
